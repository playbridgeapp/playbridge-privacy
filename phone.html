Privacy Policy for PlayBridge

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Overview

PlayBridge is a browser and TV remote control app for Android that allows you to browse the web on your phone and send video content to your TV over your local network. PlayBridge has no accounts and no developer servers: we, the developer, do not receive your personal data. The only data that leaves your device besides your own browsing and casting traffic is limited diagnostic data sent to Google by Google ML Kit, described below.

Data stored on your device

PlayBridge stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Browsing history and bookmarks — URLs and titles of pages you visit. Never sent to the developer.
  • Browser tabs — Open tab state, stored locally for session restore.
  • TV pairing information — A randomly generated auth token, the IP address/port of your paired TV, and a randomly generated identifier for this phone. Used only to connect to your own TV on your local network.
  • App settings — Playback preferences and other app configuration.
  • Downloaded media — Files downloaded via the app are stored in your device's local storage.

Network usage

  • Your TV — PlayBridge communicates with your paired TV over your local network. When connecting, it sends the TV the phone's name and its randomly generated PlayBridge identifier so the TV can recognise this phone. This data stays on your own devices.
  • Websites you browse — Standard web browsing traffic between your device and the sites you visit, and the media you choose to play or cast from them.
  • Cloud backup (optional) — If you set up Cloud Backup with your own S3-compatible storage (for example Backblaze B2), the app uploads a backup of your settings to that storage. The backup file name includes this device's Android ID so backups from different devices don't overwrite each other. The storage is yours; the developer has no access to it.

Google ML Kit (diagnostics)

PlayBridge uses Google ML Kit Language Identification to detect the language of subtitles. The language detection itself runs on your device, and subtitle text is not sent anywhere. As part of its normal operation, ML Kit sends Google the following data for diagnostics and usage analytics:

  • Device information (manufacturer, model, OS version and build)
  • App information (package name and app version)
  • Device or other identifiers, including per-installation identifiers
  • Performance metrics, API configuration, error codes and the languages identified

This data is sent over HTTPS, is used by Google only for diagnostics and analytics, and is not shared by PlayBridge with any other company. It is not sent to the developer. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / Network access — Required for web browsing and communicating with your TV over the local network.
  • Wi-Fi Multicast — Used for local network discovery (mDNS) to auto-detect your TV.
  • Photos, videos and audio — Used only to list media on your phone that you choose to cast or play.
  • Microphone — Used only to include audio while you mirror your screen to the TV, if you allow it.
  • Notifications and foreground services — Show playback, casting and download controls and keep them running in the background.

Third-party services

PlayBridge uses GeckoView (Mozilla's browser engine) to render web pages; GeckoView's own privacy policy applies to its operation. PlayBridge uses Google ML Kit as described above. PlayBridge does not use any advertising or crash reporting SDKs and does not sell your data.

Children's privacy

PlayBridge does not knowingly collect any information from children.
